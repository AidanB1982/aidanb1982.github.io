About Blackwood Publishing

Blackwood Publishing is an independent imprint dedicated to literary and psychological fiction shaped by atmosphere, place, and memory.

Our work explores the quiet forces that shape lives over time. Landscapes that remember, houses that listen, and histories that refuse to remain buried.

Rooted in the Scottish Highlands and beyond, our titles focus on isolation, recursion, and the uneasy relationship between people and the places they inhabit.

Each story stands on its own. But something connects them.
