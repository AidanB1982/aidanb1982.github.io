The Cursed Bothies

“The bothy waits.”

Deep in the Scottish Highlands stand the bothies, stone shelters built for survival in the wild. Places of refuge. Places of silence.

But some walls remember too much.

The Cursed Bothies is a series of interconnected Highland horror novels rooted in isolation, fractured memory, grief, and the quiet dread of the landscape itself.

The land watches. The weather erases. And what is buried does not stay buried.

Enter the bothy. Leave altered.

A storm traps strangers inside a remote Highland shelter. When an ancient object is disturbed, the bothy begins to change.
