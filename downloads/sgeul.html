Version 0.1.1 · approximately 42 MB. This is a testing version, ahead of the Google Play release.

Download Sgeul for Android
  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap the download button.
  2. Open Files → Downloads → Sgeul-0.1.1-debug.apk.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to install apps from this source, then tap Install. You can turn that permission off afterwards.
  4. Open Sgeul and sign in with your existing Blackwood Publishing account. If updating, install over the existing app; do not uninstall or clear its storage.

This APK is for Android phones, not iPhones. Your reading access uses your existing account and assignments. Books saved in your website browser need to be saved again inside Sgeul.

If the download does not start, try the direct file.
