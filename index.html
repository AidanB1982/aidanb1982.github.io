Corrour Bothy: The First Summoning

Some shelters protect. Others keep you.

Deep in the Cairngorms, a stone bothy waits beneath snow-laden peaks. To some, it is a refuge. To others, something less easily named.

Isobel and Thomas arrive carrying the strain of a fractured relationship. Jude and Emily come chasing folklore and half-remembered stories. Declan walks alone, untethered from the life he left behind.

Their paths converge at Corrour Bothy, where smoke rises from a chimney that should be cold, and footsteps echo where no one walks. The bothy does not ask who they are. It already knows.

Corrour Bothy is the first instalment in The Cursed Bothies, an interconnected series of Highland novels in which each remote shelter holds a fragment of something older and far less patient than the storm.
