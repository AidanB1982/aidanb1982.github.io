Corrour Bothy: The First Summoning

Some shelters protect. Others keep you.

Deep in the Cairngorms, a stone bothy waits beneath snow-laden peaks. To some, it is a refuge. To others, something less easily named.

Their paths converge at Corrour Bothy, where smoke rises from a chimney that should be cold, and footsteps echo where no one walks.

The first instalment in The Cursed Bothies, where each shelter holds something older and far less patient than the storm.
