ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Clarity before jargon.

Martin Hales is Blackwood Publishing’s accountant and the author of guides that make accounting language, everyday records and financial routines easier to understand.

His books connect the terms with the work: recording income and expenses, understanding the ledgers, reconciling the bank and preparing reliable year-end figures. Worked examples, checklists and companion resources help readers put the explanations into practice.

Whether you are learning bookkeeping, becoming self-employed or organising property accounts, the aim is the same: clearer records and a better understanding of what the figures mean.
