Highland Series

The Cursed Bothies

Remote shelters. Harsh weather. Old presences that do not stay buried.

Series Introduction

Begin in the shelter. Stay for what follows.

“The bothy waits.”

Deep in the Scottish Highlands stand the bothies: stone shelters raised against distance, weather, and exposure.

But some walls remember too much. In these stories, the land watches, the weather erases, and what is buried does not always remain still.

Each book stands on its own, but together they form a sequence shaped by isolation, recurrence, folklore, and unease.
