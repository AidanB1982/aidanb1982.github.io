Help shape Sgeul.

Every Story Begins Somewhere

We’re looking for up to 100 Android readers to help us test Sgeul before its public launch. Read, save a story for the journey, and tell us honestly what works and what needs attention.

Apply to join the beta ↓

What you’ll need

  • An Android phone running Android 7.0 or later, with Google Play.
  • A Google account you can use to join the test.
  • To be aged 18 or over.
  • Time to try the app and remain opted in for at least 14 consecutive days.

An iPhone version is not part of this test.

A small test. Useful feedback.

Try signing in, turning pages, saving a book offline, reopening in airplane mode and returning online. Let us know about confusing screens, accessibility problems and anything that breaks.

Selected testers will receive temporary in-app access to The Black Bothy during the test. No purchase or positive review is required. Existing ARC assignments remain separate.

Apply for a beta place

Applications are reviewed by Blackwood Publishing. Signing up does not reserve a place or immediately grant access. Selected readers will receive joining instructions by email.
