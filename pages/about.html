About Blackwood Publishing

Blackwood Publishing is an independent imprint dedicated to literary and psychological fiction shaped by atmosphere, place, and memory.

Many of these works began as notes taken while walking in remote parts of the Highlands. Fragments of place, people and experiences we carry forward. Altered over time, though not always willingly.

Landscapes that remember, houses that listen, and histories that refuse to remain buried.

Based in West Dunbartonshire, and rooted in the Highlands and beyond, these stories return to isolation, recursion, and the uneasy relationship between people and the places they inhabit.

Each story stands on its own. But not entirely.

All works are written under the Blackwood imprint.
