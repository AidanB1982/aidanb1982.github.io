About Blackwood Publishing

Independent fiction shaped by place, memory, silence, and the things that do not stay buried. Blackwood Publishing is home to literary, psychological, and place-haunted fiction by Aidan Blackwood.

Blackwood Publishing

Some places are not found. They are returned to.

“This is fiction that lingers.”

Blackwood Publishing is an independent imprint for stories that sit with what most people avoid: grief, memory, silence, isolation, and the things that do not stay buried.

Many of these works began as fragments. Places that hold onto what happened there. Moments that do not settle cleanly. Over time, those fragments shift, but they are never entirely left behind.

Based in West Scotland and rooted in the Highlands and beyond, Blackwood Publishing returns again and again to haunted landscapes, listening houses, histories that resist being forgotten, and the uneasy bond between people and the places that remember them.

Approach

Atmosphere before explanation.

The work is developed with a focus on atmosphere, restraint, and continuity. Stories are not treated as isolated pieces, but as part of a wider body connected by place, tone, recurrence, and memory.

Publishing remains independent, with global distribution across digital and print platforms. The aim is not volume, but consistency over time: a body of work that deepens as it grows.

Values

What guides the work.

Clarity

Work is presented without excess. What is shown is intentional, and what is withheld matters too.

Atmosphere

Place is not a backdrop. It is an active presence shaping events, memory, perception, and consequence.

Restraint

Stories do not always resolve cleanly. Meaning is not forced, and conclusions are not imposed.

Independence

Publishing decisions are made without trend, pressure, or external expectation.

Continuity

Each work forms part of a wider body, connected by tone, place, memory, and recurring themes.

Unease

The stories leave room for what cannot be neatly named. The quiet thing is often the thing that remains.
