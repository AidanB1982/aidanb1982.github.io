About Blackwood Publishing

Blackwood Publishing is an independent imprint for stories that sit with what most people avoid. Grief, memory, silence, and the things that do not stay buried.

Many of these works began as fragments. Places that hold onto what happened there. Moments that do not settle cleanly. Over time, those fragments shift, but they are never entirely left behind.

These stories move through haunted landscapes, listening houses, and histories that resist being forgotten. They are shaped as much by absence as by what is present.

Based in West Scotland, rooted in the Highlands and beyond, Blackwood Publishing returns again and again to isolation, repetition, and the uneasy bond between people and the places that remember them.

This is fiction that lingers. The kind that stays with you after you’ve stepped away.

Each story stands on its own. But not entirely.

All works are written under the Blackwood imprint.

Approach

The work is developed with a focus on atmosphere, restraint, and continuity. Stories are not isolated pieces, but part of a wider body connected by place, tone, and recurrence.

Publishing remains independent, with global distribution across digital and print platforms. The aim is not volume, but consistency over time.

Values

Clarity — Work is presented without excess. What is shown is intentional, and what is withheld is equally important.

Atmosphere — Place is not a backdrop, but an active presence shaping events, memory, and perception.

Restraint — Stories do not resolve cleanly. Meaning is not forced, and conclusions are not imposed.

Independence — Publishing decisions are made without trend, pressure, or external expectation.

Continuity — Each work forms part of a wider body, connected by tone, place, and recurring themes.
