About Blackwood Publishing

Independent voices. Extraordinary stories.

Blackwood Publishing is an independent Scottish publishing imprint committed to championing emerging, independent and overlooked authors. We bring together fiction rooted in atmosphere, memory and place, alongside carefully developed practical books and resources.

Why Blackwood exists

Our mission

To give independent voices the opportunity to be heard, stories the care they deserve, and readers new worlds to discover.

A publishing house should open doors for writers as well as introduce readers to remarkable work. That belief shapes the choices we make and the standards we are working to build.

Our approach

Books with a sense of place.

At the heart of Blackwood's fiction are the places that shape us: remote paths, isolated houses, uncertain records and the things we carry through them. Some stories draw on the landscapes of Scotland; others follow old bargains, fragile memory or the consequences of silence.

The catalogue makes room for different voices. Alongside literary, psychological and speculative fiction, Blackwood publishes clear, practical guides that help readers work confidently with everyday accounting and business records.

Whether you are meeting a book for the first time, returning to a series or looking for a practical resource, this is a place to explore the work and find your own way in.

What guides us

Our five core values

Championing independent voices is more than a line in our mission statement. These are the principles we want authors, readers and booksellers to recognise in how Blackwood operates.

  1. Every Voice Matters

    We believe great stories can come from anywhere. We champion emerging, independent and overlooked authors, giving their voices the respect, opportunity and platform they deserve.

  2. Fairness Without Compromise

    We believe authors deserve honest partnerships, fair rewards and control over their creative work. We aim to act with integrity, communicate transparently and put people before profit.

  3. Stories Worth Telling

    We value imagination, originality and craftsmanship. Every book deserves thoughtful attention, from its first words to the experience of the reader holding it or opening it on a screen.

  4. Stronger Through Community

    We believe publishing should connect people, not create barriers. We build meaningful relationships between authors, readers, independent bookshops and the communities that bring stories to life.

  5. Progress With Purpose

    We embrace innovation that makes publishing fairer, reading more accessible and independent authorship more sustainable. Technology should serve stories and people, never overshadow them.

These values guide the way we develop future submission opportunities, publishing agreements and author programmes. Individual rights, royalties and publication terms must always be explained in a written agreement; our values statement does not replace those terms or guarantee publication.
