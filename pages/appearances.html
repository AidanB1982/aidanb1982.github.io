The calendar is used to request an initial discussion slot. Larger events, in-person appearances, paid engagements, festivals, and bookshop events may require further confirmation by email before they are considered final.

How to Request an Appearance

Step One

Send Event Details

Use the Blackwood contact form to send the organisation name, event type, proposed topic, location, audience size, links, and any relevant dates.

Step Two

Request a Time

Use the booking calendar to request an available discussion slot. This is for the initial conversation, not automatic confirmation of a full event.

Step Three

Confirm by Email

If the event is suitable, details can then be confirmed by email, including format, timing, links, travel, fees, or promotional needs.
