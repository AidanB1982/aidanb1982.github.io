Bookshop discovery

Find independent bookshops

Search a UK town or city to discover bookshops. Review each result before saving it to your directory.

Discovery uses OpenStreetMap and Blackwood-curated bookshop data. Results may be incomplete or outdated. No shop is contacted or published automatically.

Bookshop quality control · Manual review

Verify bookshops before outreach

Review the public discovery candidates one at a time. Save promising shops to your private browser directory, then record whether they are independently operated, still trading and supported by evidence. Verification is not consent to email and does not establish Blackwood stockist status.

Open the private review queue to browse candidates.

Candidate records are public OpenStreetMap discovery data. They are not verified independents, contact permissions or Blackwood stockists. Private review notes stay in this browser and remain in your JSON backup.

Relationship management · Private planning

Bookshop follow-up planner

Use your existing saved reminders and contact history to decide what needs attention next. No messages are sent, drafted or scheduled by this dashboard; every outreach action requires your separate review.

These are planning reminders, not permission to contact. Bookshops marked Do not contact, closed, not independent or confirmed stockist are excluded from follow-up prompts. Later-dated reminders remain in their saved records. Everything stays in this browser and its JSON backup.
