Publishing With Blackwood

Acquisition means something here.

Blackwood Publishing is an independent Scottish publishing house. We select books we believe we can publish with conviction, care and a clear understanding of the work required to bring them to readers.

We do not acquire manuscripts simply to fill a publishing schedule. Every book accepted by Blackwood must have a reason to belong on the list and a publishing case that we are prepared to stand behind.

House Principles

What Acquisition Means

I

Authors are not our customers.

Authors whose work is acquired by Blackwood do not pay us to publish it. We do not charge a reading or consideration fee.

II

The author retains copyright.

Blackwood acquires only the publishing rights expressly agreed in the author publishing agreement. Rights not granted to Blackwood remain with the author.

III

Blackwood invests in the books it acquires.

The house bears the normal financial and operational risk of publication and funds the agreed editorial, design, production and publishing work required to bring an acquired book to market.

Acquisitions

What We Look For

A manuscript does not need to resemble another Blackwood book. It does need to give us a compelling reason to publish it.

Quality

Work with a strong foundation and the potential to reach Blackwood's publication standard.

Conviction

A book we can genuinely support and champion beyond its publication date.

Reader

A work with an identifiable readership and a clear reason for that readership to care.

Editorial Fit

A work that belongs within the Blackwood publishing programme.

Contribution

A book that strengthens the list, develops an audience or author relationship, or brings meaningful editorial value.

Partnership

An author relationship that can work constructively through editorial development, production, publication and the longer life of the book.
