Our Author Charter

Good publishing begins with respect for the people who write the books.

Blackwood Publishing champions emerging, independent and overlooked authors. Our ambition is to create opportunities for distinctive work while remaining clear about what we can publish, which rights are involved and what a fair working relationship requires.

The principles stated in our acquisitions policy

What Blackwood stands behind today

Our existing acquisitions page sets out three principles for manuscripts we consider and books we acquire.

01

No pay-to-publish arrangement

We do not charge authors a manuscript reading or consideration fee. Authors whose books we acquire do not pay us to publish them.

02

Respect for copyright

The author retains copyright. Blackwood receives only the publishing rights expressly agreed in a signed publishing agreement; rights not granted remain with the author.

03

Publisher responsibility

For books Blackwood acquires, the house carries the ordinary financial and operational risk and funds the editorial, design, production and publishing work agreed for that title.

The standards we are building

Fairness must be understandable.

As we prepare to consider outside manuscripts, we are developing the processes and agreement standards needed to put our five values into practice. The following are aims for that work, not claims that every system or term is already in place.

Clear rights and agreements

Prospective authors should be able to understand which formats, territories and periods a proposed licence covers, what rights remain theirs and how any rights-reversion provisions operate before signing.

Understandable earnings

Proposed royalties, calculation bases, deductions, reporting arrangements and payment terms should be explained in writing before agreement. We will not advertise a fixed royalty rate until one has been approved for the relevant arrangement.

Editorial care and communication

Authors deserve a considered process and clear expectations about editorial work, decisions, responsibilities and communication. Each open submission window should publish its scope and an expected response period.

Accessible, responsible submissions

Before opening the desk, we aim to make instructions easy to follow, explain how manuscript information is handled and provide a suitable, secure route for sending work. A submission should not require authors to surrender rights simply to be considered.

For writers considering Blackwood

The manuscript desk is currently closed.

We're preparing the secure intake, editorial capacity and published submission guidelines for a future reading window. There is no open application or manuscript upload route at present. Please do not send manuscripts through the general contact form.

When a window opens, the Submissions page will confirm eligible work, requested materials, submission rules, the submission method and the expected response timeframe. A submission is an opportunity for consideration, not a guarantee of acceptance or a publication contract.

About this charter

This page describes our publishing principles and the standards we intend to develop. It is not a contract, an offer to publish or a statement of agreed royalty percentages. The specific rights, obligations, payment terms and responsibilities for any acquired work must be set out in an individual written agreement. Future digital-reading or author programmes may use separate terms and onboarding processes.
