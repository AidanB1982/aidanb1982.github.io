The Archivists

Read. Record. Earn. Return.

The Archivists is Blackwood Publishing's private reader community. Create your Reader Record, collect Archive Points through eligible Blackwood activity, access private material, and redeem available rewards through the Blackwood Store.

01

Become an Archivist

Create your free Reader Record to access the Private Archive and keep track of your points, rewards, redemptions, reading activity, and private Blackwood updates.

02

Build Your Record

Submit Reader Records, purchase through the Blackwood Store, respond to private dispatches, and take part in eligible Blackwood activity to build your Archive Points total.

03

Keep Your Collection

Keep a private record of the Blackwood books you own or have read, with personal notes alongside each title.
