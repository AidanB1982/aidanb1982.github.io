Independent Works

Rooted in place, memory, and psychological tension.

These works stand alone, shaped by atmosphere and the quiet persistence of what remains.

Dour Hill House
When Clara inherits Dour Hill House, she hopes for a new beginning. Instead, her daughter whispers to the mirror and something whispers back.
“A haunting tale… intense and atmospheric.”
The Red-Clad Collector
Every Christmas, a child disappears. No footprints. No explanation. Just one less memory.
“Creepy, unsettling, and stays with you.”
Love, Abused
A work of narrative nonfiction examining how a child’s life becomes a record. What is observed, documented, and ultimately missed.

Love, Abused contains references to childhood neglect, abuse, and systemic failure. It is not graphic but may be emotionally difficult for some readers.
