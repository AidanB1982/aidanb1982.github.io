A Little About the Book

Accounting for UK Landlords

Rental Income, Expenses, Tax Records, Self Assessment and Making Tax Digital
by Martin Hales | Kindle Edition

Managing rental property is much easier when every figure has a clear trail — from the rent received to the final Self Assessment return.

Accounting for UK Landlords is a practical, step-by-step guide to recording rental income, expenses, deposits, letting-agent transactions, mortgages, jointly owned properties, capital costs and year-end tax information.

Written for individual UK landlords rather than accounting specialists, the book follows Sophie and Daniel through a complete accounting case study, showing the full source-to-return audit trail from individual transactions and reconciliations to annual tax calculations and next-year opening balances.

  • Distinguish rent, deposits, owner funding and capital receipts
  • Record gross rent and letting-agent deductions correctly
  • Classify repairs, improvements, domestic items and shared costs
  • Separate mortgage interest from mortgage principal
  • Calculate property profit and residential finance-cost tax reductions
  • Prepare reliable records for Self Assessment and Making Tax Digital
  • Manage joint ownership, arrears, advance rent and owner-paid expenses
  • Close a tenancy, transfer or sell a property and preserve Capital Gains Tax records

Whether you manage one rental property or a small portfolio, this book provides a disciplined system for keeping accurate records, spotting mistakes early and approaching your tax return with confidence.
