A Little About the Book

Accounting for UK Sole Traders

A Practical Guide for Business Owners and Students
by Martin Hales | Kindle Edition

Accounting does not need to feel like a second business you never meant to start.

Accounting for UK Sole Traders is a practical guide for business owners and students who want to understand the records, routines and figures behind a small UK sole trader business.

Starting with the basics, this book explains how income, expenses, drawings, VAT, payroll, bank reconciliation, year-end figures, Self Assessment and Making Tax Digital fit together. Each topic is built around clear explanations, worked examples, practice tasks and checklists, so you can see how everyday transactions become reliable accounts.

Whether you are running a small business, starting self-employment, studying bookkeeping, or trying to make sense of your own records, this guide gives you a steady route through the essentials without unnecessary jargon.

  • Keep accurate business records
  • Record income, expenses, cash payments and refunds
  • Understand drawings, profit and taxable profit
  • Deal with VAT, payroll and year-end adjustments
  • Prepare figures for Self Assessment
  • Build better bookkeeping habits throughout the year

A clear, practical workbook-style guide for anyone who wants better records, more confidence and a stronger understanding of sole trader accounting in the UK.
